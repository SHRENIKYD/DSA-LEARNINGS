Privacy policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026. This policy covers the Interview Mastery website (shrenikyd.github.io/DSA-LEARNINGS) and the Interview Mastery apps for Android and iOS.

The short version

  • You can use everything without an account. Then your progress stays on your device.
  • If you create an account, we store your email address and your learning progress so it syncs between devices.
  • No ads and no analytics or tracking tools. The only thing sent automatically is an anonymous crash report (see Error reports below). We never sell or share your data for advertising.
  • You can delete your account and its data at any time, in the app or on the web.

Data we collect

If you create an account

  • Email address and an account ID, to sign you in.
  • Password, if you sign up with email. It is stored only as a secure hash by our sign-in provider; we cannot see it.
  • Name and profile picture, if you choose "Continue with Google". Google shares these with us; we use the picture only to show it next to your email.
  • Learning progress: which topics, problems, quizzes and interview sets you have ticked as done, and the last lesson you opened, so you can resume on any device.

Stored only on your device

  • Progress when you are not signed in, play-mode stats (XP bonuses, streak days, daily quests, game wins), and settings such as theme, sound, haptics and reading speed. These are kept in your browser or app storage and are not uploaded.
  • Read aloud uses your device's own text-to-speech engine. Lesson text is spoken on your device; nothing is recorded, and the app never uses the microphone.

What we do not collect

No location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera, advertising ID, or usage analytics (only the anonymous error reports described below).

Error reports

When the website or app hits an unexpected error, it sends a short anonymous report so we can fix the crash.

  • What is sent: the app (web or android), the version, the page, the error message, the technical stack trace, and your browser or device description (user agent). Email-like text and anything after a "?" in a link is removed before sending, and the fields are length-limited.
  • What is not sent: no account ID, no email address, no cookies. We do not store your IP address; our database provider may see it as part of normal network traffic.
  • Where: our Supabase database. Only we can read it.
  • How long: reports older than 90 days are deleted by a weekly cleanup, so none is kept longer than about 97 days.
  • Opt out: nothing is sent if the setting imastery.noErrors is set to 1 in your browser's local storage (in the browser console: localStorage.setItem('imastery.noErrors','1')). No reports are sent when accounts and Supabase are not configured or when you are offline.

Why we use it

Only to run the service: to sign you in, sync and show your progress, and keep your account secure. We do not use your data for advertising or profiling.

Who processes it

  • Supabase hosts accounts and the progress database. Each person can read and change only their own progress (database row-level security), and all traffic uses HTTPS.
  • Google handles "Continue with Google" if you use it. The website also loads fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when fonts download.
  • YouTube (Google) plays the lesson videos. Nothing is loaded from YouTube until you tap a video; it then plays in YouTube's privacy-enhanced player (youtube-nocookie.com), and YouTube receives your IP address and viewing activity under Google's privacy policy.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the website and keeps standard web server logs, such as IP addresses.

We do not sell, rent or share your personal data with anyone else.

Delete your account

  • In the app: Me → Delete account.
  • On the web: shrenikyd.github.io/DSA-LEARNINGS/login.html?delete=1, sign in, then confirm.

Deleting removes your account, email and all synced progress immediately and permanently. Data stored only on a device is removed from that device when you delete or sign out there; you can also clear it by uninstalling the app or clearing site data.

Download your data

You can download a copy of your data at any time: on the sign-in page (web) or in Me → Download my data (app). It is a JSON file with your account email and ID (if signed in), the progress saved on our server (fetched fresh) and the progress and play stats saved on your device. If you are not signed in, it contains only the data on your device. In the Android app the data is shown as text for you to copy and save.

How long we keep data

Account data is kept until you delete your account. After deletion, a copy may remain in our database provider's automatic backups for a limited time until those backups are replaced.

Children

Interview Mastery is made for students and professionals preparing for technical interviews and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top and, for important changes, show a notice in the app.
