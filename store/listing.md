## Full description (max 4000)
Interview Mastery turns interview prep into a game you want to open every day.

Every concept starts with a real-world analogy, comes alive in a step-by-step visualizer, and ends with graded practice and interview questions. Then play mode keeps you coming back: earn XP, keep your streak, clear daily quests and beat the boss at the end of every level.

13 TRACKS, BEGINNER TO EXPERT
• DSA + Math for CS (28 modules)
• Java, Python, JavaScript & TypeScript, C++
• SQL & databases
• Low-level design and high-level system design
• Frontend and backend engineering
• GenAI for engineers
• CS core: operating systems and networks
• Behavioral interviews

194 modules, about 4,250 practice problems and 2,100 visualizers.

LEARN BY PLAYING
• 26 concept games, new puzzle every play: algorithms (binary search, Big-O, two pointers, BFS, Dijkstra, heaps, DP, trees), SQL, Java, JavaScript, Python and networking
• A new random puzzle every time you play
• Track map with a boss fight at the end of each level
• XP, levels, daily quests, streaks with freezes, and badges

STUDY YOUR WAY
• Works offline: every lesson is inside the app
• Read aloud with speed and voice controls
• Quizzes with instant feedback, interview Q&A and practice arenas
• Light and dark themes, sound effects and haptics you can switch off

SYNC ACROSS DEVICES
Sign in with email or Google to sync progress between the app and the website. Or use everything without an account; your progress then stays on your phone.

Free. No ads. No tracking.
